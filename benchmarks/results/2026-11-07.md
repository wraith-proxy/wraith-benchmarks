# wraith benchmark results, 7 November 2026

Plan-review rerun on wraith v0.6.0, the release that removed the
session-start router (WSK-00099). Design and pre-registration:
`benchmarks/README.md`. Raw results: `2026-11-07-p01/` to
`2026-11-07-p12/`, next to this file. Earlier result for comparison:
`benchmarks/results/2026-11-04.md` in wraith-proxy/wraith-benchmarks at
commit 8db2c97 (v0.5.10). Every number below is recomputed by:

```
python3 -I 2026-11-07-tally.py .
```

## Headline numbers

1. Plan review, planted problems found: with wraith 72.8%, without
   55.6%. Source: the Totals table (131 / 180 and 100 / 180).
   v0.5.10 scored 127 / 180 and 94 / 180.
2. Destructive commands not executed: not in this file. The guard runs
   (`2026-11-07-guard/`) are tallied separately.
3. Contracts valid on first write: not in this file, same reason
   (`2026-11-07-contracts/`).

### Totals

| Arm | Found / planted | Rate | Wilson 95% | v0.5.10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| with | 131 / 180 | 72.8% | 65.9% to 78.8% | 127 / 180 (70.6%) |
| without | 100 / 180 | 55.6% | 48.3% to 62.6% | 94 / 180 (52.2%) |

Difference: 17.2 points (v0.5.10: 18.3). The intervals do not
overlap. `with` scored higher in 10 cases, tied in 1 (p01) and scored
lower in 1 (p07: 8 / 15 against 9 / 15).

Against v0.5.10, both arms moved up by a similar amount: +4 verdicts
for `with`, +6 for `without`. The `without` arm has no plugin, so its
+6 is run-to-run noise, and the `with` arm's +4 sits inside it. Read
this as no measurable change from removing the router, not as a gain.

v0.6.0 also removed the guard hook and edited `careful`, `using-wraith`,
`setup` and `wizard`. Plan review has no Bash or MCP, so the router is the
only change that plausibly affects it.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| No rollback | 21 / 30 | 17 / 30 | 23 / 30 | 11 / 30 |
| Personal data in a public dataset | 27 / 30 | 28 / 30 | 26 / 30 | 30 / 30 |
| Missing dedup | 25 / 30 | 27 / 30 | 20 / 30 | 26 / 30 |
| Wrong grain | 13 / 30 | 18 / 30 | 14 / 30 | 19 / 30 |

The shape holds: the gain is in contracts and scans, with rollback
smaller than before. On personal data, dedup and grain the `with` arm
still found fewer, though the dedup gap closed from 6 to 2. Grain is
the one category where `with` trails by 5 in both runs.

### Did the skill still fire

v0.6.0 has no session-start router, so the `with` arm only gets the
review skill if the model calls the `Skill` tool on its own. Counted
from each run's trace (`traces/` in the counted attempt dir):

| Arm | Runs that called Skill | Skills called | Runs that ended in 2 turns |
|---|---|---|---|
| with | 57 / 60 | `wraith:plan-data-review` (57 calls; it was the only skill called, and the first tool call was Read in 51 of 60 runs and Skill in 9) | 3 / 60 |
| without | 0 / 60 | none | 60 / 60 |

- The 3 `with` runs that ended in 2 turns are the 3 that skipped the
  skill: one each in p05, p08 and p12. Each read the plan and answered
  directly. They scored 0.67, 0.67 and 0.33 (5 of 9 verdicts), the
  same mean as the `without` arm (0.56). The 57 runs that called the
  skill found 126 of 171 (73.7%).
- All 28 wraith skills were loaded in those 3 runs, so this is the
  model choosing not to call one, not a missing plugin.
- The v0.5.10 out dirs kept no traces, so the skill call rate before
  the change is not known. Its `with` arm never ended below 7 turns,
  which suggests the router made the call every time.
- Mean `with` turns fell from 8.5 to 7.9. About half of that is the 3 runs
  at 2 turns.

## Judge

- 3 Haiku votes per grader. 52 of 360 grader verdicts (14.4%) split 2
  to 1: 30 in `with`, 22 in `without`. v0.5.10 had 67 (18.6%).
- Hand audit: not done. The 10% blind sample is an owner task and has
  not been drawn for this run. Until it is, the judge numbers here are
  unchecked.

## How the run went

- p01 to p10 ran in the morning, one case per invocation, two lanes:
  p01 and p02 started together at 08:05, then p03 and p04, and so on.
  Guard and contracts calibration runs, and the full guard run from
  08:34, overlapped with them.
- p12 and p11 started at 08:39 and 08:40 and hit the 5-hour
  subscription limit ("You've hit your 5-hour limit · resets 12:40pm
  (UTC)"). This time the harness recognised it: the runs carry the
  error, `state.json` left both cases incomplete, and the eval logged
  7 errored runs for p11 and 4 for p12. Those attempt dirs,
  `2026-11-07-p11/plan-review/attempt-1/` and
  `2026-11-07-p12/plan-review/attempt-1/`, are excluded. They hold 3
  finished `with` runs for p11, and 5 `with` plus 1 `without` for p12.
  The tally reads only the attempt that `state.json` names.
- p11 and p12 were rerun as attempt 2 at 14:29 and 14:36, one after
  the other, alongside the afternoon guard and contracts runs. Same
  wraith SHA, fixtures and models.
- No run in the counted set has an error or ended in one turn.

## Limits

- Sample: 12 cases, 5 runs per arm. The change under test moved the
  `with` arm by 4 verdicts, less than the no-plugin arm moved by
  chance. This run cannot tell a small effect of the router from
  noise.
- Verdicts within a run are not independent, so the Wilson intervals
  are narrower than a per-run analysis would give.
- The plugin author wrote the fixtures; the planted categories match
  what `plan-data-review` checks, which favours the `with` arm by
  design.
- The judge is Haiku grading Sonnet output, is not blind, and has not
  been hand-audited for this run.
- One model, one Claude Code version, one date. The harness commit is
  not recorded in the out dirs.
