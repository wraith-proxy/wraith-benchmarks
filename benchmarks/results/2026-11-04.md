### By planted category

Each category is planted 6 times, so 30 verdicts per arm. Category is
the grader name suffix (`1-dedup` is dedup).

| Category | with | without |
|---|---|---|
| Missing contract field | 27 / 30 | 4 / 30 |
| Unpartitioned full scan | 17 / 30 | 4 / 30 |
| No rollback | 23 / 30 | 11 / 30 |
| Personal data in a public dataset | 26 / 30 | 30 / 30 |
| Missing dedup | 20 / 30 | 26 / 30 |
| Wrong grain | 14 / 30 | 19 / 30 |

The gain comes from three categories: contracts, scans and rollback.
On personal data, dedup and grain the `with` arm found fewer. One
reading: the skill's checklist pulls attention to its own items. This
was not tested further.

### Secondary: invalid issues

Not measured. The pre-registration lists issues the judge rates as

planted-problem graders per case, so no run has an invalid-issue
verdict. Recorded here rather than added after the results were seen.

## Judge

- 3 Haiku votes per grader. 67 of 360 grader verdicts (18.6%) split 2
  to 1.
- Hand audit: the owner judged 36 of 360 verdicts (10%) blind, with
  arm and judge verdict hidden, and disagreed with the judge on 5
  (13.9%). In 4 the judge failed a finding the owner would pass (2 per
  arm); in 1 (`with` arm) the judge passed one the owner would fail.
  On the owner's verdicts the sample moves by +1 for `with` and +2 for
  `without`: the judge reads slightly strict, with no sign of bias toward an
  arm in 5 disagreements.
- Sampling: seed 20261104 drew 36 items. 8 of them (items 11 to 18)
  were answered with the judge's verdict visible, so they were not
  blind and were dropped. Seed 202611042 drew 8 replacements (items
  37 to 44) from the 324 items not yet shown. Key and answers:
  `2026-11-04-audit/key.json` (all 44 drawn, including 11 to 18) and
  `2026-11-04-audit/answers.json` (the 36 counted). Score:

## How the run went

- r2 ran all 12 cases in one process. p06 to p12 hit the 5-hour
  subscription limit after p05 and were scored 0. The harness did not
  recognise the message "You've hit your 5-hour limit" and marked the
  run done. Fixed in `20ff8ea` (WSK-00065). Those 70 runs are excluded;
  they stay in `2026-11-04-r2/` and `tally.py` skips them.
- p06 to p12 were rerun the same day with the fixed harness, one case
  per invocation, two lanes in parallel. Fixtures, answer keys, wraith
  tag and models are unchanged, so the pre-registration still holds.
- No run in the counted set has an error or ended in one turn.

## Limits

- Sample: 12 cases, 5 runs per arm. Only large differences show
  outside the intervals. Verdicts within a run are not independent, so
  the Wilson intervals are narrower than a per-run analysis would give.
- The plugin author wrote the fixtures; the planted categories match
  what `plan-data-review` checks, which favours the `with` arm by
  design.
- The judge is Haiku grading Sonnet output and is not blind. The hand
  audit above is the check.
- One model, one Claude Code version, one wraith tag, one date.
- Only plan review ran. Guard and contracts are not measured.
